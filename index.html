About

Jason Baillie is a Northern Australian Guitarist. Born and Bred in Darwin, Northern Territory and Shaped by Townville, North Queensland's Regional Hub, Jaosn is a songwriter and independant thinker known for blending genres such as Blues, Rock and Heavy Metal with Cinematic storytelling and futuristic themes across multiple local and international identies.

One of the last five music students to graduate from James Cook University Townsville, Jason has performed across multiple Australian bands including Bolverk, Mojo Cover Band, Katie Richards and the Details, and the nationally recognised act King Social, plus many other peripatetic supporting guitarist opportunities.

King Social gained national attention through Australia’s Got Talent in 2015, Triple J Unearthed and winners of the Passport to Airlie Beach Festival of Music, combining Australian storytelling with country, reggae, hip-hop and rock influences.

During King Social's tenure, Jason expanded into an international collaboration with former Megadeth members Glen Drover and Shawn Drover, and Firewind vocalist Henning Basse, through a sci-fi inspired online Thrash Metal Songwriting project exploring space exploration and the unknown. This was a project that Jason had always wanted to complete years ago and slowly completed each song in the night hours of his spare time amongst lifes responsibilities.
