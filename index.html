About

Jason Baillie is a Northern Australian Guitarist. Starting life in Darwin, Northern Territory and Shaped by Townsville, North Queensland's Regional Hub, Jason is a songwriter and guitarist known for blending genres such as Blues, Rock, Hip-Hop and Heavy Metal from Cinematic storytelling to futuristic themes across multiple local and international music collaborations.

Graduating with a Bachelor of Music from the School of Creative Arts (SOCA), James Cook University, Townsville. Jason has performed in local Australian bands including Luke Hietamaki, Katie Richards, and the nationally recognised act King Social, plus many other supporting guitarist roles.

King Social gained national attention through Australia’s Got Talent in 2016, Triple J Unearthed and winners of the Passport to Airlie Beach Festival of Music, combining Australian storytelling with country, reggae, hip-hop and rock influences. Fun-loving hip-hop/indie quintet King Social scored no. #3 showing on debut with the single Big Man in 2016's Carlton Dry Independent Music Charts.

During King Social's tenure, Jason worked on an online international collaboration with former Megadeth members Glen Drover and Shawn Drover, and Firewind vocalist Henning Basse, through a sci-fi inspired online Thrash Metal Songwriting project exploring the concept of space exploration and the unknown. This was a project that Jason worked on to complete over a 5 year period and slowly completed each song in the late night hours of his spare time amongst other life commitments.
